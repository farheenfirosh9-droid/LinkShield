About LinkShield

LinkShield is a beginner-friendly cybersecurity awareness project designed to help people notice common warning signs in website addresses.

It uses HTML to structure the page, CSS to create the interface, and JavaScript to inspect URL patterns and show a result.

The tool is intended for learning and awareness. It does not visit the submitted website, inspect its content, or query a live phishing or malware database.

Remember: a clean result is not proof that a link is safe. When in doubt, use the official website or app.

Online Safety Tips

Use these habits whenever you receive a link by email, text message, social media, or chat.

✓

Check the Real Domain

Read the hostname carefully. Misspellings or extra words can imitate a familiar brand.

✓

HTTPS Is Not a Guarantee

The padlock indicates an encrypted connection, not that the website itself is trustworthy.

✓

Be Careful with Short Links

Shortened links can hide the destination. Confirm the sender and destination before opening.

✓

Question Urgent Messages

Scammers may create panic with account warnings, delivery problems, prizes, or deadlines.

✓

Protect Passwords and OTPs

Never share passwords, one-time codes, PINs, or recovery codes with someone who contacts you.

✓

Use Official Channels

Open the official app or type the known website address yourself instead of using an unexpected link.

✓

Check Attachments and QR Codes

Be cautious with unexpected files, QR codes, payment requests, and links from unknown senders.

✓

Keep Devices Updated

Update your browser, operating system, and security software to reduce exposure to known threats.

✓

Check the Link Destination

A link's visible text may differ from its actual address. Check the destination before proceeding.

✓

Pause Before Paying

Verify bank details and payment requests independently, especially when the request is unexpected.

Clicked a Suspicious Link? Act Quickly.

1

Stop interacting. Close the page. Do not download files, approve prompts, or enter more information.

2

Secure your accounts. If you entered a password, change it through the official site and enable multi-factor authentication.

3

Get help if needed. Contact your bank immediately if payment details were exposed, and report the message to the relevant service.
